## s2.3 Failure to Start on Demand
An emergency set that fails a start test has lost its protective function. Check
starting batteries under load, fuel supply and the air inlet restriction
indicator first. An emergency generator that will not start is treated as a
critical defect regardless of whether the site is currently on mains power.

## s2.6 Black Smoke and Power Loss
Black exhaust smoke with reduced output indicates incomplete combustion, most
commonly a restricted air filter (FLT-AIR-C18) or failing injectors (INJ-C18).
Check inlet restriction before condemning injectors. Injector replacement is a
long-lead item.

## s4.1 Coolant Temperature High
Sustained coolant temperature above 98 C causes cylinder head damage. Verify
coolant level, radiator cleanliness and thermostat operation. Shut down on high
temperature alarm rather than running to failure.

## s7.2 Fuel or Oil Leakage
Any visible fuel or oil leak on a generator set is both a fire risk and an
environmental release risk. Contain immediately, isolate the set and rectify
before the next start.
