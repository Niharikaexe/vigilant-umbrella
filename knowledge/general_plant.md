## s2.1 Conveyor Belt Tracking and Damage
A belt running off-centre wears the edge and can tear. Check idler alignment and
material loading before adjusting tracking. A torn belt (BELT-PC1200) stops
material flow but is not usually a safety issue unless personnel access the
pinch points.

## s2.4 Conveyor Emergency Stop Integrity
A pull-cord or emergency stop that does not stop the belt is a safety-critical
defect. The conveyor must not run until it is restored.

## s3.2 AHU Filter Differential Pressure
Filter differential above the design limit collapses the filter and contaminates
the supply air. In a paint hall this causes finish defects. Replace the bag
filter set (FLT-BAG-DV150).

## s3.6 AHU Fan Bearing Vibration
Rising fan bearing vibration precedes failure by weeks. Trend it. Above
7.1 mm/s RMS, plan a bearing change at the next window.

## s4.3 Mooring Winch Brake Holding Capacity
A mooring winch brake that renders below its design holding load is a severe
safety hazard: a parted or released line under tension has a lethal snap-back
zone. Take the winch out of service and re-test the brake holding capacity.

## s4.6 Winch Hydraulic Leakage
Hydraulic leakage at the quayside is both a slip hazard and a release to water.
Contain immediately.
