## s4.2 Hoist Brake Performance
Any increase in load drift after the brake sets, or a grinding noise during
braking, means brake pad wear or a contaminated brake disc. A crane that drifts
under load is unsafe to operate and must be taken out of service immediately.
Replace pads as a set (BRK-PAD-40T). A statutory thorough examination by a
competent person is required before returning the crane to service after any
brake defect.

## s4.5 Wire Rope Discard Criteria
Discard the rope when broken wires exceed 10 in any 6d length, when there is
localised diameter reduction greater than 7%, when corrosion pitting is visible,
or on any evidence of core protrusion or kinking. Rope defects are safety
critical: no lifting operation may continue. Replacement rope is WIRE-ROPE-24.

## s4.9 Travel Drive Vibration
Vibration or juddering during long travel typically indicates wheel flange wear,
rail misalignment or a failing gearbox bearing. Measure wheel-to-rail clearance
and check gearbox vibration signature. Above 7.1 mm/s RMS, plan an intervention
at the next window; above 11 mm/s RMS, stop the crane.

## s6.1 Limit Switch Faults
An unresponsive or intermittent hoist limit switch removes a primary safety
protection. The crane must not be operated until the limit switch is proven
functional. This is not deferrable to a planned window.
