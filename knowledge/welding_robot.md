## s2.4 Weld Seam Deviation
Progressive deviation of the weld seam indicates a torch neck deformation
(TORCH-1520), a shifted tool centre point, or worn axis gearboxes. Recalibrate
the tool centre point before replacing mechanical parts. Deviating welds on
structural joints are a quality escape and must be quarantined.

## s3.1 Axis Brake Fault
A brake fault on any axis means the arm can drop under gravity when powered
down. The cell must be isolated and no one may enter the working envelope until
the brake is proven. This is a safety-critical fault.

## s4.2 Wire Feed Instability
Irregular wire feed causes porosity and spatter. Check liner wear, drive roll
pressure and the contact tip before investigating the feeder motor.

## s5.5 Fume Extraction Interlock
If the fume extraction interlock is bypassed or faulty, welding must stop.
Operator exposure limits are exceeded within minutes without extraction.
