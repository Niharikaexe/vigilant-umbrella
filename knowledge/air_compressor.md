## s3.2 High Element Outlet Temperature
Element outlet temperature above 110 C trips the unit. Common causes are low oil
level, a blocked oil cooler, a failed thermostatic valve, or ambient temperature
above design. Repeated high-temperature trips shorten element life materially.
The compressor element (CMP-ELEM-90) is a long-lead item.

## s3.5 Excessive Oil Carry-Over
Oil in the downstream air line indicates a saturated or ruptured separator
element, an overfilled sump, or a blocked scavenge line. Oil carry-over
contaminates downstream tooling and paint processes and must be corrected before
the air is used for coating work.

## s4.1 Unloaded Running and Short Cycling
Frequent load/unload cycling wastes energy and wears the inlet valve. Check for
system leaks, an undersized receiver, or an incorrectly set pressure band.

## s6.3 Condensate Drain Failure
A failed condensate drain releases oily water. This is an environmental release
and must be contained and reported.
