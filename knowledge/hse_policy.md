## s2.1 Reporting of Injuries and Near Misses
Any signal mentioning an injury, a near miss, or a person being struck, caught
or exposed is reported to the HSE duty officer before any maintenance action is
planned. Maintenance priority is never allowed to delay the safety response.

## s9.1 Lock-Out/Tag-Out for Safety-Critical Defects
Before work begins on an asset with a safety-critical defect, energy sources are
isolated, locked and tagged, and a permit to work is issued. An automated system
may prepare the permit but may never issue it without a named competent person.

## s6.2 Spill Containment
On any identified or suspected release of oil, fuel, coolant or chemicals,
containment is deployed first and the environmental coordinator is informed.
Releases to water carry a statutory reporting obligation.

## s4.4 Work at Height and Confined Space
Any intervention on roof-mounted plant, inside tanks, or in voids requires the
corresponding permit and a standby person. These jobs are never auto-scheduled.
